Grammar practicePráctica de gramática

Past perfect and past perfect continuousPasado perfecto y pasado perfecto continuo

This week you will practice the past perfect and past perfect continuous to establish an accurate clinical timeline for patients with herpes zoster (shingles). Esta semana practicarás el pasado perfecto y el pasado perfecto continuo para establecer una cronología clínica precisa de pacientes con herpes zóster (culebrilla).

The past perfect shows which of two past events happened first, which is essential in documentation, incident reports, and handovers, where a single change in tense can alter a medication or symptom timeline. El pasado perfecto muestra cuál de dos eventos pasados ocurrió primero, algo esencial en la documentación, los informes de incidentes y las entregas de turno, donde un solo cambio de tiempo verbal puede alterar la cronología de un medicamento o un síntoma.

Past perfect (had + past participle) for an action completed before another past event
By the time the patient arrived, the rash had spread to his back.
Contrast with the past simple for events in sequence
When I arrived, the patient took his antiviral (after I arrived). When I arrived, the patient had taken his antiviral (before I arrived).
Past perfect continuous (had been + -ing) for duration up to a past point or for a visible past result
She had been experiencing burning pain for four days before she was admitted.
Negatives and questions: hadn't + past participle; Had + subject + past participle?
The patient hadn't received the varicella vaccine. Had he started antivirals before the surgery was postponed?
